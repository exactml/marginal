A review loads the rules and decisions your team accepted from
`.marginal/knowledge.yaml`, read from the PR's base commit: the ones for this
repo about a changed file, a file in its folder, or a folder holding it, at
most 15, newest first. A change that breaks a rule or reverses a decision gets
a finding that names the pull request it came from. Without the file, a review
is unchanged; a bad entry is skipped with a warning.
