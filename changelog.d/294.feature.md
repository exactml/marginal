New `review.max_reviews_per_pr` setting (default 20) caps how many times
marginal reviews one pull request. Once a PR has that many reviews, a later
push gets no model call: marginal posts one notice saying the limit was
reached, and raising the setting in `.marginal/config.yaml` allows more.

([ISSUE-294](https://github.com/exactml/marginal/issues/294), [PR-304](https://github.com/exactml/marginal/pull/304) by [@exactml](https://github.com/exactml))
